Bitcoin

Bitcoin is the original digital currency, born from the 2008 whitepaper and launched in early 2009 as the first practical blockchain.

  • 2008: Satoshi Nakamoto publishes the Bitcoin whitepaper.
  • 2009: Genesis block mined; first peer-to-peer cryptocurrency begins.
  • 2010: First real-world transaction buys pizza for 10,000 BTC.
  • 2013–2017: Price rallies, mainstream awareness grows, and institutional interest rises.
  • 2021–2024: ETF approvals, broader adoption, and continued focus on Bitcoin as digital gold.

Ethereum

Ethereum introduced programmable smart contracts and decentralized applications in 2015, making blockchain much more than money.

  • 2013: Vitalik Buterin publishes the Ethereum whitepaper.
  • 2015: Mainnet launches, enabling smart contracts and decentralized platforms.
  • 2016: The DAO hack tests community governance and leads to the Ethereum/Classic split.
  • 2020: DeFi and NFTs drive explosive network usage.
  • 2022: The Merge shifts Ethereum from Proof of Work to Proof of Stake.

Cardano

Cardano focused on research-driven development, building a scalable PoS blockchain with a strong emphasis on academic review and sustainability.

  • 2017: Cardano launches with a peer-reviewed, proof-of-stake protocol called Ouroboros.
  • 2019: Shelley upgrade introduces decentralization through staking.
  • 2021: Smart contracts arrive with the Alonzo upgrade.
  • 2022–2024: Growth centers on governance, scalability, and global research partnerships.

Binance Coin

Binance Coin started as the native token for the Binance exchange and evolved into a utility token across trading, fees, and the Binance Smart Chain ecosystem.

  • 2017: BNB is launched during Binance’s initial token sale.
  • 2019: Binance Coin becomes a key fee-discount token on the Binance exchange.
  • 2020: Binance Smart Chain launches, expanding BNB’s use cases into DeFi and NFTs.
  • 2023: BNB remains central to transaction payments, staking, and protocol development on the BNB Chain.

Solana

Solana emerged as a high-speed blockchain for DeFi and NFTs, focusing on performance and low transaction costs while growing quickly in the early 2020s.

  • 2020: Solana mainnet launches with a novel proof-of-history consensus design.
  • 2021: Rapid adoption driven by DeFi protocols and digital art marketplaces.
  • 2022: Network reliability becomes a major focus as Solana handles heavy traffic and occasional outages.
  • 2023–2024: Continued ecosystem growth around memecoins, gaming, and scaling improvements.
